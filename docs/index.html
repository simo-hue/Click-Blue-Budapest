02

The Plan

Two steps, starting with one platform in front of BME.

Step 1

One platform, brought back to life

  • Rebuild and extend the platform in front of BME, ringed with floating green plants.
  • Open up the beach upstream: cleaned, greened, with stairs down to the water.
  • A club that looks after the place and runs organised watersports with local sports clubs.
  • School workshops that bring the river into the classroom.
  • Public toilets at street level, open to everyone.
Step 2

Quiet, and room to grow

  • A see-through noise barrier of plants and walls along the street, made from sustainable materials. Open to all, not a private area.
  • One more floating dock, built with a system proven on the Danube.
  • New uses on the water, such as a fitness area.
03

Our Impact

  1. 01.Economic. New blue jobs, tourism and sponsorship.
  2. 02.Social. A community space that reconnects citizens with the Danube.
  3. 03.Environmental. A cleaner, greener riverbank, and a generation of schoolchildren who know their river.
